Introduction

My name is Muhammad Shoaib, and I am a third-year BS English Language Teaching (ELT) student at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro. As part of my academic program, I completed an 80-hour teaching internship at Ideal Public High School. This internship provided me with an opportunity to gain practical experience and understand the working environment of a school.

During the initial days of my internship, I observed experienced teachers and learned about their instructional approaches, lesson delivery, and classroom activities. These observations helped me understand how teachers present lessons and deal with students of different learning abilities. Gradually, I became involved in classroom activities and started performing my assigned responsibilities.

Throughout the internship, I participated in teaching activities, helped students understand their lessons, and assisted teachers in maintaining a positive learning environment. I also observed how teachers used textbooks, examples, questions, and discussions to make lessons more effective.

This experience enhanced my understanding of teaching practices, improved my communication and presentation skills, and increased my confidence. It also helped me connect my academic knowledge with practical classroom experience and develop a better understanding of my future profession as an English language teacher.

INTERNSHIP OBJECTIVES

The primary purpose of my 80-hour teaching internship at Ideal Public High School was to develop professional teaching abilities and gain firsthand experience of the educational environment. The internship was designed to help me understand the practical aspects of English language teaching and the learning needs of school students.

My major objectives were

  1. To understand the structure and daily functioning of a school environment.
  2. To develop the ability to prepare and organize effective lesson plans.
  3. To practice teaching English grammar, vocabulary, reading, and writing skills.
  4. To learn how to explain difficult concepts through simple language and relevant examples.
  5. To understand the different learning abilities and educational needs of students.
  6. To explore teaching aids and classroom resources for improving the learning process.
  7. To develop skills in asking questions, evaluating student understanding, and providing constructive feedback.
  8. To build professional ethics, responsibility, patience, and confidence as a future English language teacher.

Through these objectives, I aimed to strengthen my teaching abilities, improve my professional knowledge, and gain the practical skills required for a successful career in English Language Teaching.
